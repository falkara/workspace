`Colorimetry` mixes a color from the light of others, as a surface is white with a tint of another color. `mix({ base, tint, share, luminance })` is a `Definition` that, in each medium, takes the base and the tint in that medium, mixes their tristimulus values as CSS Color 5 `color-mix()` mixes them in `xyz-d65`, `share` being the percentage of the tint over 100, scales the mixture until its relative luminance is `luminance`, so that the share sets the chromaticity and the luminance the lightness, and proves it in the gamut of the medium; mixing is linear in light, so the result is exact. `white` defines the white of each medium, its channels all at 1, the white its standard writes in a standard medium. `MixAmounts` checks the share, from 0 to 1, and the luminance, above 0 and at most 1, that of the white, each with its reason, and reads them from JSON. The luminance is a `RelativeLuminance`, Y relative to the white as WCAG 2.2 defines it, written with `relativeLuminance` and branded apart from a value relative to the white, which bounds a linear channel. A mixture that leaves the gamut at its luminance fails in that medium with an `OutOfGamut`, and one whose base and tint mix to black with a `BlackMixture`.
