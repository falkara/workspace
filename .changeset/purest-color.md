`Colorimetry.purestColor` turns a hue into a color in a medium: the purest color of the hue the medium shows, at the highest luminance a headroom allows, as exact tristimulus values. In the medium, the linear channels of a color α·W + β·D of the hue are α·w + β·d, and the purest is the one with the largest β/α at which no channel is below 0, found in closed form, exactly in `Rational`. Every measure of purity CIE defines grows with β/α along a hue, so the result does not depend on which one is meant. Where the direction lowers no channel, the gamut holds the spectral light itself, and the purest color is that light alone: in Rec. 2020 and BT.2100, whose primaries are rounded coordinates, the gamut contains the spectral lights of 532 nm and 630 nm. The headroom bounds the largest linear channel relative to the white rather than the luminance Y; it is 1 by default, so that in HDR a color stays at the level of HDR Reference White, and may reach the peak of the medium, up to 10000/203 for PQ. `PurestColorOptions` refuses a headroom that is not positive or that exceeds the peak, each with its reason.
